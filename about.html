A small studio that ships useful things.

TalkingScientist Ventures LLC is a product studio registered in Arkansas. We design, build, and maintain a small catalog of software tools and digital products for small businesses and tradespeople.

Who we build for

Our customers run shops, crews, and storefronts. They don’t have an IT department, and they don’t want another subscription that needs babysitting. They need tools that work the first time, do one job well, and stay out of the way.

That shapes everything we make: store and web apps for the platforms they already use (Shopify, WordPress, Chrome), and operational workbooks for the trades, starting with our GritBench line.

How the studio operates

We keep the studio deliberately small and run it on heavy automation. Build pipelines, testing, and support tooling handle most of the repetitive work, which is how a small operation keeps an entire catalog maintained instead of abandoned.

A few things we hold ourselves to:

  • No abandonware. Products are maintained or clearly retired, never left to rot in a marketplace listing.
  • Honest claims. Product pages describe what a tool does today, not a roadmap dressed up as a feature list.
  • Real support. Every product routes to support@talkingscientistventures.com, and mail there gets read and answered.
  • Payments handled by professionals. Checkout runs through established marketplaces and payment platforms. We never see or store your card details.
