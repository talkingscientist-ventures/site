Privacy Policy

Effective August 24, 2026 · TalkingScientist Ventures LLC, Arkansas, United States

The short version: we collect almost nothing. No ad tracking, no analytics, no marketing cookies, and we never see your payment card details. Here’s the whole of it.

1. What we collect

Support email

If you write to support@talkingscientistventures.com, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it to answer you and to fix the problem you reported. Correspondence is kept as ordinary business records.

Server logs

Like nearly every website, our web server records basic technical logs: IP address, browser type, pages requested, and timestamps. We use them only to keep the site running and secure, and they are routinely deleted on the hosting provider’s standard rotation schedule.

That’s the complete list. This site sets no tracking cookies, runs no analytics scripts, and serves no advertising.

2. What we don’t collect

  • Payment details. Purchases run through third-party marketplaces and payment platforms (for example Etsy, app marketplaces, or a merchant-of-record checkout). They process your payment under their own privacy policies; card numbers never reach us.
  • Behavioral or advertising data. We don’t track you across the web, build profiles, or buy or sell personal data. Ever.

3. Third parties

Three kinds of third parties touch small amounts of data on our behalf:

  • Marketplaces and payment platforms you buy through, under their own policies. They typically share your order details (not card details) with us so we can deliver and support the product.
  • Our email provider (Google Workspace), which hosts our support mailbox.
  • Our web host (DreamHost), which serves this site and generates the server logs described above. This page loads its two fonts from Google Fonts, which involves your browser requesting the font files from Google’s servers.

We don’t share personal information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

4. Your choices and rights

If you’ve corresponded with us and want your messages deleted, email support@talkingscientistventures.com and we’ll delete them, unless we’re legally required to keep a record (for example, records tied to a transaction). Depending on where you live, you may have additional legal rights over your personal data; write to the same address to exercise them, and we’ll respond.

5. Children

Our products and this site are for businesses and working adults, not children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from children.

6. Changes

If our practices change — for instance, if we ever add analytics — we’ll update this policy first and change the effective date at the top. The current version always lives at this address.

7. Contact

Privacy questions go to support@talkingscientistventures.com, or by mail to TalkingScientist Ventures LLC, 701 South St Ste 100, Mountain Home, AR 72653, United States.
