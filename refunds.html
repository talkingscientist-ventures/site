Refund Policy

Effective August 24, 2026 · TalkingScientist Ventures LLC, Arkansas, United States

Our products are sold through third-party marketplaces and payment platforms, and each channel’s own refund process governs the transaction. Here’s how that works in practice — and how we help.

1. The general rule

Because a marketplace or merchant of record processes every payment, refunds are issued through the channel you bought from, under that channel’s policy:

  • Etsy (our GritBench shop): refund requests go through Etsy’s order help flow, and Etsy’s policies for digital downloads apply.
  • App marketplaces (such as the Shopify App Store, WordPress.org-distributed plugins with paid upgrades, or the Chrome Web Store): the marketplace’s refund rules and billing system apply.
  • Direct checkout via our merchant of record: the merchant of record processes the refund; start by emailing us and we’ll arrange it.

2. Where we stand

Digital goods can’t be “returned,” and most channels reflect that in their default policies. Even so, our position is simple: if a product doesn’t do what its listing says it does, we want you to have your money back. When a channel’s process gives us discretion over a refund request, that’s the standard we apply.

3. How to ask

  1. Start with the channel you bought through (its receipt or order page links to its refund process), or email us first if you’re not sure — either is fine.
  2. Email support@talkingscientistventures.com with the channel, your order number, and what went wrong.
  3. We’ll respond within two business days — often with a fix, and with the refund routed through the right channel when a refund is the right answer.

4. What refunds can’t cover

  • Purchases made somewhere we don’t sell — if someone re-sold you our product against our terms, we can’t refund what we never received, though we’d like to hear about it.
  • Requests outside the window a channel’s policy allows, where the channel gives us no discretion.

5. Changes

If we add sales channels or our practices change, we’ll update this page and its effective date. The current version always lives at this address.
